NUS Hardware Acceleration and Reconfigurable Computing · Fall 2026 · with Xiong Binzhi

LDPC Decoder Accelerator on AMD Kria K26

7.85× faster than the Cortex-A53 214 ms per 256×192 image 0 bit mismatch vs. reference 2957 → 892 cycles per iteration

A Normalized Min-Sum decoder for a custom QC-LDPC(384,194) code, written in C++ with Vitis HLS and packaged as an AXI-Stream IP. In the Vivado block design it sits behind AXI DMA; a bare-metal Cortex-A53 controller sets up the transfers, starts the IP and times each image. Three hardware versions were measured on the board against a plain-C A53 baseline, all decoding the same fixed noisy image and producing bit-identical output.

System

  • HLS: v2 partitions memories by the QC structure and parallelizes check/variable updates; v3 adds parallelism and runs the PL at 125 MHz
  • Firmware: standalone A53 controller with AXI DMA, hardware timer and a JTAG mailbox
  • Host: Python/XSDB tools that program the board, upload LLRs and verify every output bit

Extension of the LDPC project · AMD ZCU111 RFSoC · Kria K26

LDPC-Coded Photo over a Real RF Channel

303 real channel bit errors corrected 15 decoder iterations to an exact image FPGA output bit-identical to reference

A pixel-art photo is Huffman-compressed, LDPC-encoded and sent as a QPSK waveform from the ZCU111 DAC through an SMA cable into its ADC. Lowering the DAC power produces real channel errors. The PC synchronizes and demodulates the capture, and the LLRs are decoded by the HLS decoder on the Kria. Drag the slider to watch the errors disappear iteration by iteration.

NUS research project · Aug 2026 – present · AMD ZCU111 RFSoC

RFSoC Clock Synchronization and Frequency Compensation

  • Measuring relative clock drift and communication latency between a ZCU111 and a PC over Ethernet.
  • Comparing board-side and PC-side timestamps over 2–90 s to estimate the relative clock rate in ppm, and testing whether a fixed transmission-delay compensation holds across measurement durations.
  • Working toward a PC-as-master architecture that estimates and separately compensates the frequency offsets of two Ethernet-connected RFSoC boards.
